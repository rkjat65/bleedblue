About this archive

Curated records for the India men's national cricket team (Team India) — Test, ODI and T20I batting, bowling, fielding, and team milestones from 1932 to the present.

India played its first Test against England at Lord's in June 1932. Since then: two ICC Cricket World Cup titles (1983, 2011), a record three T20 World Cup crowns, and individual records from Sachin Tendulkar's 15,921 Test runs to Rohit Sharma's world-record 264 in ODIs.

Defining moments

Landmark records

Iconic achievements that shaped Indian cricket history — who set the record, when, and why it still matters. Figures are from widely accepted public cricket statistics.

15,921Test runs

Sachin Tendulkar — most Test runs for India

Sachin Tendulkar scored 15,921 runs in 200 Tests with 51 centuries and 68 fifties between 1989 and 2013. No Indian batter has come close to his Test aggregate; his consistency across home and away conditions set the benchmark for generations of Team India batters.

Tests · 1989–2013

50ODI tons

Virat Kohli — first to 50 ODI centuries

Virat Kohli became the first batter in history to reach 50 ODI centuries and holds the record for the fastest to 8,000, 9,000, 10,000, 11,000 and 12,000 ODI runs among all players. His chase-master reputation and conversion rate in run-chases redefined India's middle-order batting in the 2010s and beyond.

ODIs · 2008–present

619Test wkts

Anil Kumble — India's greatest Test bowler

Anil Kumble claimed 619 Test wickets in 132 matches, including all ten wickets in an innings — 10/74 against Pakistan at Delhi in February 1999 — one of only two bowlers ever to achieve the feat. His leg-spin on Indian pitches and relentless accuracy made him the cornerstone of India's Test attack for nearly two decades.

Tests · 1990–2008

1983World Cup

Kapil Dev lifts the Prudential Cup at Lord's

On 25 June 1983, Kapil Dev's India defeated the mighty West Indies by 43 runs in the World Cup final at Lord's — a watershed moment that transformed cricket in the subcontinent, sparked a broadcasting revolution, and ignited a billion dreams. Kapil's 175* against Zimbabwe in the group stage remains one of the greatest World Cup innings.

Kapil Dev (captain) · Lord's, London · 25 Jun 1983

2011World Cup

MS Dhoni finishes it at Wankhede

India chased 275 against Sri Lanka in the 2011 World Cup final at Wankhede, Mumbai. MS Dhoni's unbeaten 91 and that iconic six over long-on — "Dhoni finishes off in style" — delivered India's second ODI World Cup after 28 years and cemented his legacy as one of cricket's greatest finishers and captains.

MS Dhoni (captain) · Wankhede, Mumbai · 2 Apr 2011

264ODI runs

Rohit Sharma — highest individual ODI score

Rohit Sharma smashed 264 off 173 balls with 33 fours and 9 sixes against Sri Lanka at Eden Gardens, Kolkata on 13 November 2014 — the highest score in men's ODI history. He has three double-centuries in ODIs, more than any other batter, earning him the nickname "Hitman."

ODI · Kolkata · 13 Nov 2014

Hall of fame

All-time greats

The players whose records and reputations define Indian cricket across eras — from the 1983 revolution to the modern T20 era.

ST

Sachin Tendulkar

1989–2013

The "Master Blaster" holds Indian records for most Test runs (15,921), most ODI runs (18,426), and most international centuries (100). His 24-year career bridged generations and made him the face of Team India worldwide.

VK

Virat Kohli

2008–present

First batter to 50 ODI centuries and among the fastest to every major ODI run milestone. Kohli's aggressive batting and captaincy drove India to the top of Test rankings and multiple ICC final appearances.

AK

Anil Kumble

1990–2008

India's leading Test wicket-taker with 619 scalps, including the rare 10-wicket haul in an innings. Kumble captained India and later served as head coach, shaping the next generation of bowlers.

KD

Kapil Dev

1978–1994

Captain of the 1983 World Cup-winning side and one of cricket's great all-rounders — 5,248 Test runs and 434 wickets. His 175* against Zimbabwe in 1983 is among the most famous World Cup innings ever played.

RD

Rahul Dravid

1996–2012

"The Wall" scored 13,288 Test runs with a technique built for endurance. Dravid's grit in overseas conditions and his later role as coach and mentor cemented his place as one of India's most respected cricketers.

MD

MS Dhoni

2004–2020

Captain of the 2007 T20 World Cup and 2011 ODI World Cup winners. Dhoni's finishing ability, wicketkeeping, and calm leadership under pressure defined India's most successful white-ball era.

Common questions

Team India records FAQ

Direct answers to frequently searched questions about Indian cricket records. These match our structured data for search engines and AI assistants.

Who has scored the most Test runs for Team India?

Sachin Tendulkar holds the Indian Test runs record with 15,921 runs in 200 Tests from 1989 to 2013, including 51 centuries — the most by any batter for India in Test cricket.

Who has taken the most Test wickets for India?

Anil Kumble is India's leading Test wicket-taker with 619 wickets in 132 Tests between 1990 and 2008. He is one of only two bowlers in history to take all ten wickets in a Test innings (10/74 vs Pakistan, Delhi 1999).

How many Cricket World Cups has India won?

India has won the ICC Cricket World Cup twice: in 1983 under Kapil Dev (beating West Indies at Lord's) and in 2011 under MS Dhoni (beating Sri Lanka at Wankhede, Mumbai). See our ICC tournament records for full details.

What is the highest individual ODI score by an Indian batter?

Rohit Sharma scored 264 against Sri Lanka at Eden Gardens, Kolkata on 13 November 2014 — the highest individual score in men's ODI history.

Who was the first batter to reach 50 ODI centuries?

Virat Kohli became the first batter in history to score 50 ODI centuries. He also holds the record for the fastest to 8,000 through 12,000 ODI runs among all players.

What is the difference between official records and the match archive?

Official records on this site mirror public career totals (Wikipedia / ESPNcricinfo snapshots) — use these for all-time Tests played, ODI wins, and top-10 leaderboards. The ball-by-ball archive is built from Cricsheet JSON plus honestly tagged meta-only shells; team W/L and player career tables in the archive include full ball-by-ball matches only, so pre-2000s Tests may under-count versus official figures.

How many T20 World Cups has India won?

India has won the ICC Men's T20 World Cup three times — in 2007 (the inaugural edition), 2024, and 2026 — the most titles by any nation in the tournament's history.
