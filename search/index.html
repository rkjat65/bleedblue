Search Team India Cricket Records

Free records site for the India men's national cricket team (Team India / Men in Blue). Explore official career win–loss, batting and bowling leaders, ICC World Cup and T20 World Cup titles, captaincy history, series results, match centre and player profiles at cricket.rkjat.in.

Official career results (public sources, mid-2026)

  • Tests: 599 matches — 186 won, 188 lost, 224 draws, 1 tie (~31.13% win rate).
  • ODIs: 1,078 matches — 574 won, 450 lost, 10 ties, 44 no results (~55.99% win rate).
  • T20Is: 283 matches — 187 won, 80 lost, plus super-over wins (~66.08% win rate).

ICC trophies

  • Cricket World Cup champions: 1983, 2011
  • T20 World Cup champions: 2007, 2024, 2026 (most titles)
  • Champions Trophy: 2002 (shared), 2013, 2025
  • World Test Championship: finalists 2021 and 2023

All-time leaders (India)

Most Test runs: Sachin Tendulkar (15,921), Rahul Dravid, Sunil Gavaskar, Virat Kohli.

Most Test wickets: Anil Kumble (619), Ravichandran Ashwin, Kapil Dev, Harbhajan Singh.

Most ODI runs: Sachin Tendulkar (18,426), Virat Kohli, Sourav Ganguly, Rohit Sharma.

Highest ODI individual: Rohit Sharma 264 vs Sri Lanka (Kolkata, 2014).
